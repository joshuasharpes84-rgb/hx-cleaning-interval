› Cleaning Interval & Bundle Life — CaCO₃-Scaling Cooler on Temperature Control Method & code operations tool

A shell-and-tube cooler on cooling water, with a temperature controller throttling the water. As CaCO₃ scale builds, the controller opens the water valve until it runs out. Paste or upload logged temperatures and flows: the tool backs out U(t) by ε-NTU inversion, fits falling-rate, linear and asymptotic fouling trends, and projects when the valve saturates, with a confidence cone. It then finds the cost-optimal cleaning interval (Peters & Timmerhaus cyclic-operation framework, adapted to lost duty) and how many hydroblast cycles the carbon-steel bundle survives on Uhlig's pitting factor. Key finding from the demo data: the trend form, not the arithmetic, sets the answer.
