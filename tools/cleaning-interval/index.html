Cleaning Interval & Bundle Life

Cooling-water cooler on temperature control: as CaCO₃ scale builds, the controller opens the water valve until it runs out. Your logged data projects when that happens (with a confidence cone), what cleaning on that schedule costs, and how many hydroblast cycles the carbon-steel bundle survives. Method, equations and sources: README.md.

Fouling trends — sorted by which needs cleaning soonest

Falling rate is the prediction (fitted to your data, with a confidence cone). Linear and asymptotic are the bounds: fouling that never slows, and fouling that levels off. Open a row to see its equation and edit its parameters.

Temperature / shear-coupled fouling sensitivity Scenario

Scenario only, not a plant prediction. All three trends use one shared clean-U basis and the same calculated fouling threshold. The asymptote is calibrated to its fitted R*f and τ; the generated fouling resistance—not flow-induced changes in U—is refitted. Process inlet variation recalculates controlled CW flow, wall temperature and shear. See README §3.5 for assumptions.
